# Launch page rubric

Scored by a reviewer who sees only screenshots of each section at desktop (d-NN.png, 1440 wide) and phone (m-NN.png,
390 wide), plus docs/FACTS.md. PASS or FAIL per criterion, with one concrete fix for each FAIL.

1. **First screen sells it.** Within a few seconds a visitor can say what Glade is (a scene behind the Claude Code
   terminal, plus an owl that alerts you) and what to click.
2. **Hierarchy and flow.** One clear primary action; sections build in a sensible order; each has one job.
3. **Claims.** Every factual claim on the page appears in docs/FACTS.md. Flag any claim that does not (price, social
   proof, "free", signing, privacy beyond the sheet, features that do not exist). Accurate claims only.
4. **Copy.** Plain, specific, friendly, no filler, no pronouns for Hoot other than "it", sentence case.
5. **Legibility.** Text is readable over the scene at both widths (size, contrast); nothing is cut off, overlapping, or
   awkwardly wrapped.
6. **Phone layout.** No horizontal scrolling, comfortable tap targets, sections stack sensibly, nothing cramped.
7. **Visual identity.** Consistent pixel-art style (palette, square cards, the owl), the scene backdrop supports rather
   than fights the text; Hoot is the memorable element.
8. **Motion and states.** Based on what a screenshot can show: the hero demo reads, mood states are clear.
