# UX profile: Glade launch page

Read by the VectorLab UX audit. These are the project's deliberate choices; anything not listed here follows the
catalog hard rules.

- **Aesthetic:** playful pixel art. Dark plum canvas, cream text, one yellow accent (CTAs, step rules, focus), Hoot the
  owl as the memorable element. Not "Stripe".
- **Voice:** plain, friendly, specific. Hoot is "it". Sentence case. Verb-first buttons.
- **Contrast target:** WCAG 2.1 AA (4.5:1 body). **Hit targets:** 44x44.
- **Text floor:** 12px; body 16px, secondary 14px.

## Deliberate exceptions to the catalog (brand, not oversight)

1. **Hard offset "pixel" shadows** on cards (`shadow-[8px_8px_0_...]`). Catalog says soft shadows only. This is the
   square, pixel-art card signature shared with the app and Hoot's bubble. Cards still carry a 1px-equivalent border.
2. **Display weight 800 (Bricolage Grotesque) on headings.** Catalog caps at 600. The chunky display face is the brand;
   body and UI text stay at 400-600.
3. **A decorative firefly loop on the fixed backdrop.** Catalog bans infinite decorative loops. It is a stepped, low-
   amplitude blink on a background layer, approved by the owner, and `prefers-reduced-motion` removes it.
4. **Product-demo timings** (bubble pop 270 ms, typing 20 ms/char) copy the real app so the demo is honest; they are
   demo content, not page chrome. Chrome transitions use the 100/150/200/300 ms tokens.

## Motion rules for anything new

- Chrome: transform/opacity only, 100/150/200/300 ms, ease-out in, ease-in out, no bounce.
- Motion graphics (video) are muted, loop, show a static poster under `prefers-reduced-motion`, and carry a text
  equivalent next to them. Meaning is never carried by motion alone.

## Typography note (learned the hard way)
Pixelify Sans is only used at 24px+ (the two big call-to-action buttons). At 14-16px its letterforms blur in browsers
("Claude" read as "Qlaude", digits as 8s), so all small UI text stays in Bricolage Grotesque / JetBrains Mono. The macOS app,
which renders at 2x with crisp metrics, uses it down to 14pt for buttons and small labels.
